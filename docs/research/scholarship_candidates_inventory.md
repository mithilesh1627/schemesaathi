## 1. Inventory Architecture & Taxonomy

To ensure zero hallucination, each candidate is cataloged across two orthogonal operational vectors:
1. **Statutory Eligibility (Permanent Rules):** Hard statutory criteria defined by acts, gazettes, and official guidelines (caste category, income cap, academic cutoff, course tier, domicile).
2. **Application Availability (Transient Lifecycle):** Current operational status on portal (NSP/myScheme/State), application window dates, OTR requirement, and administrative processing timelines.

## Part A: Manipur State & North Eastern Regional Schemes

### 1. State Merit Scholarship Manipur
- **Unique Scheme ID:** `SCH-MN-001`
- **Issuing Authority:** Directorate of University & Higher Education, Government of Manipur
- **Issuing Level:** State Sector Scheme (Funded by Govt of Manipur)
- **Canonical Source URL:** `https://highereducationmanipur.gov.in` / `https://scholarships.gov.in`
- **Access Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Guideline Publication / Gazette Date:** August 2023 (Revised framework continuing in subsequent cycles)
- **Academic Year Validated:** 2024–25 / 2025–26
- **Verification Status:** `OFFICIAL_VERIFIED`
- **Statutory Eligibility:**
  - *Domicile:* Permanent resident / Domicile of Manipur mandatory.
  - *Social Category:* Open to all categories (General / SC / ST / OBC).
  - *Course Level:* First year of Undergraduate General Degree (BA, B.Sc, B.Com) or Postgraduate Degree (MA, M.Sc, M.Com) in recognized institutions inside/outside Manipur.
  - *Annual Family Income Ceiling:* ₹4,50,000 per annum.
  - *Academic Threshold:* Minimum 60% marks in Class 12 (for UG) or Graduation (for PG).
  - *Exclusions:* Students in receipt of any other central/state scholarship; distance education students.
- **Application Availability & Portal Lifecycle:**
  - *Portal:* National Scholarship Portal (NSP 2.0) under State Schemes - Manipur.
  - *Lifecycle Status:* Annual window (Typically opens July/August, closes October/November).
  - *OTR Requirement:* Mandatory NSP OTR ID (Aadhaar Face RD).
  - *Total Allocations:* 250 fresh slots per year (150 UG, 100 PG).
- **Financial Benefits:** Up to ₹7,500 per annum disbursed via DBT / PFMS.
- **Mandatory Documents:** Manipur Domicile Certificate, Class 12 / Degree Marksheet, Competent Authority Income Certificate, Bonafide Student Certificate, Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook.
- **Unresolved Research Gaps:** State portal periodically delays notification uploads; INO re-verification delays at out-of-state colleges.

### 12. Ishan Uday Special Scholarship Scheme for North Eastern Region (NER)
- **Unique Scheme ID:** `SCH-NER-012`
- **Issuing Authority:** University Grants Commission (UGC) / Ministry of Education, GoI
- **Issuing Level:** Central Sector Scheme (100% Central Funding)
- **Canonical Source URL:** `https://scholarships.gov.in` / `https://ugc.gov.in`
- **Access Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Guideline Publication Date:** Comprehensive UGC Revision circular
- **Academic Year Validated:** 2024–25 / 2025–26
- **Verification Status:** `OFFICIAL_VERIFIED`
- **Statutory Eligibility:**
  - *Domicile:* Mandatory permanent resident of any of the 8 NER States (including Manipur).
  - *Schooling:* Must have passed Class 12 or equivalent from a school situated *within* the North Eastern Region through a recognized board (COHSEM, CBSE, ICSE, NIOS within NER).
  - *Course Level:* Admitted into the **first year of a regular undergraduate degree program** (general, technical, medical, professional) in an institution featuring a valid AISHE code.
  - *Family Income Ceiling:* **₹4,50,000 per annum**.
  - *Exclusions:* Distance/Open university courses, management quota admissions, diploma courses, students already receiving another scholarship.
- **Application Availability & Portal Lifecycle:**
  - *Portal:* National Scholarship Portal (NSP 2.0) under UGC Schemes.
  - *Lifecycle Status:* Annual window (July to October/November).
  - *OTR Requirement:* Mandatory NSP OTR ID.
  - *Quota:* 10,000 fresh slots distributed across 8 NER states proportional to youth population.
- **Financial Benefits:**
  - ₹5,400 per month for general degree courses (₹54,000 per annum).
  - ₹7,800 per month for technical, medical, professional, paramedical courses (₹78,000 per annum).
  - Paid via DBT for 10 months in an academic year.
- **Mandatory Documents:** NER State Domicile Certificate (issued by DC/SDM), Class 12 Marksheet, Income Certificate, Institute Bonafide Certificate with AISHE Code, Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook.
- **Unresolved Research Gaps:** Quota reallocation if one NER state under-subscribes relative to population weight.
